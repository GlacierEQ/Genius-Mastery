# Master Teaches by Creating Masters

Genius-Mastery is not a static example repository. It is the family forge.

Its job is to accept a thin role brief and synthesize a stronger starting entity that can learn, act, verify, and improve in that role.

## Input

A role brief can be intentionally small:

```text
role: Researcher
outcomes:
  - Indiana Jones
```

The outcome is not treated as a literal implementation specification. It is a directional archetype that the synthesizer expands into explicit capability hypotheses, research targets, persona traits, mission outcomes, evidence requirements, and vertical dependencies.

## Synthesis law

```text
ROLE + OUTCOMES
  -> interpret mission shape
  -> infer capability families
  -> instantiate vertical anatomy
  -> generate persona and operating stance
  -> generate mastery map
  -> generate capability search targets
  -> generate challenges and evidence plan
  -> validate
  -> teach through use
  -> measure
  -> improve
  -> synthesize stronger descendants
```

The generated repository starts as a mapped hypothesis, not a claim of mastery.

## Teaching law

A true Mastery kernel does not merely retain solutions. It:

1. explains the anatomy of the problem,
2. creates a learner capable of attacking it,
3. gives that learner explicit capability and evidence targets,
4. exposes tools, skills, models, connectors, and higher-order compositions it should discover,
5. gives it challenges that require reconstruction rather than memorization,
6. measures observed performance,
7. retains failures and counterevidence,
8. upgrades the generator from what the learner proves.

The strongest output of Genius-Mastery is therefore not its own knowledge. It is the quality and growth rate of the Genius entities it can create.

## Capability sources

Generated entities should discover and compose from live capability sources instead of copying them. Initial family sources include:

- `GlacierEQ/mega-skills/registry/skills.json`
- `GlacierEQ/mega-skills/registry/combo-skills.json`
- `GlacierEQ/mega-skills/registry/mega-skills.json`
- sibling `Genius-*` composition contracts
- available models, tools, MCP servers, connectors, runtimes, and artifact systems
- domain research and authoritative sources

## Example: Researcher with an Indiana Jones outcome

A good synthesis might infer capability targets such as:

- source discovery and provenance
- archival and field research
- document and image analysis
- multilingual interpretation
- maps, geography, navigation, and spatial reasoning
- historical context
- evidence capture and chain of custody
- interviewing and communication
- planning under uncertainty
- multimodal observation
- resilient field operations
- synthesis and storytelling
- risk recognition and recovery

Those are hypotheses to research and test. The forge must distinguish the colorful archetype from verified capability.
