# Teaching Plan: Genius-Mastery

## What Mastery teaches

Mastery teaches the method of building an entity that can understand, act, verify, recover, improve, and teach within a purpose.

## Teaching sequence

1. Start from a role and desired outcomes.
2. Decompose the outcome into the complete vertical anatomy.
3. Identify which layers are mission-relevant instead of treating every detail equally.
4. Discover existing skills, Mega Skills, tools, models, connectors, runtimes, and sibling Genius capabilities.
5. Build only the missing bridges needed to create a stronger composition.
6. Execute challenges that require the learner to reconstruct the method.
7. Verify actual effects and preserve evidence.
8. Transfer the method to a new case.
9. Have the learner teach another learner.
10. Use failures and transfer gaps to improve the forge.

## Mastery test

A descendant is stronger when it can independently reconstruct and transfer verified capability, not when it can merely repeat the generator's text.
